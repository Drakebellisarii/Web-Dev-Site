# PRODUCT.md — DPBellisari WebDev Portfolio

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio and client-acquisition site for Drake Bellisari, a freelance web developer and designer. The site functions as a living credential — it IS the proof of craft, not just a list of work. Every section must feel like the output of someone who builds at a level above the market.

## Users
- **Primary:** Potential clients (small business owners, startup founders, recruiting firms) evaluating whether to hire Drake. They form a judgement in under 10 seconds.
- **Secondary:** Peers and collaborators assessing technical and aesthetic range.
- **Tertiary:** Employers or agencies reviewing the portfolio.

## Register
brand

## Brand & Tone
Editorial. Measured. Confident without being loud. The aesthetic is a slow-scrolling literary magazine crossed with a design studio — not a tech startup, not a freelancer template. Every typographic choice, every animation, every line of copy should feel like it was put there on purpose.

Anti-references:
- Generic freelancer portfolio (skills list, circular headshot, "hire me" button)
- SaaS marketing pages (neon gradients, identical metric cards, hero with floating screenshots)
- "Creative agency" clichés (full-bleed video autoplay with no content, cursor follower on mobile, excessive parallax)
- Dark blue tech aesthetic

## Strategic Principles
1. The site earns trust before it sells anything.
2. Restraint is not timidity — it is the loudest signal of confidence.
3. Typography and spacing do more work than decoration.
4. Every animation must have a reason beyond "it looks cool."
5. Mobile is not a reduced version — it is its own considered layout.

## Key Sections
- **Hero** — Full-bleed video background, name at editorial scale, scroll marquee
- **Manifesto** — Typography-driven statement of philosophy
- **Steps (§04)** — Scroll-driven sticky approach walkthrough (01–04)
- **Process (§03)** — Skills and stack: Frontend, Backend, Cybersecurity, Systems, Languages
- **Projects** — 4 case-study panels with video previews
- **Contact** — Dark section with form and channel links

## Constraints
- React + Vite + Framer Motion
- No component library — all CSS is hand-written
- Smooth scroll via Lenis
- Custom cursor (desktop only), custom scrollbar (desktop only)
